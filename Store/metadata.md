# App Store metadata

Character limits: name 30, subtitle 30, promotional text 170, description
4000, keywords 100 (comma-separated, no spaces needed). Promotional text
can be changed anytime without app review; everything else needs a
version submission.

Category: primary Lifestyle, secondary Utilities.
Age rating: 4+. Copyright: © 2026 Filip Havlík.

Privacy Policy URL (required): <https://norrill.github.io/svatek-dnes/>
Support URL (required): <https://norrill.github.io/svatek-dnes/>

Both point at the same page – it carries the policy in cs/en/de and the
support contact. The page is served by GitHub Pages from `docs/` on
`main` (see the README), so it is edited and deployed like the rest of
the repo. App privacy label: **Data Not Collected** – no data types
declared, because nothing reaches the developer or a third party. Since
1.1 the first names of matched contacts are synced to the user's own
paired Apple Watch; syncing between the user's own devices is not
collection, so the label stays.

## English

**Name:** Svátek dnes

**Subtitle** (26/30): Czech name days & holidays

**Promotional text** (147/170):

> Whose name day is it today? Track Czech name days and public holidays for your contacts – now on Apple Watch too. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

**Keywords** (79/100):

```
name day,nameday,czech,calendar,svatek,jmeniny,holiday,widget,reminder,contacts
```

**Description:**

```
Whose name day is it today? Open the app – or just glance at the widget or your watch – and you know.

Svátek dnes follows the Czech civil name day calendar and matches it with your contacts, so you never forget to send your wishes. It even recognises Czech diminutives: Káťa is Kateřina, Pepa is Josef, Klárka is Klára.

WHAT IT DOES
• Today's name days and Czech public holidays on one screen
• Your contacts' name days – including nicknames and diminutives
• Manual assignment for contacts it cannot match automatically
• Full-year calendar with name search
• Public holidays and notable days, movable feasts included
• Morning notifications at your chosen time, plus an evening-before reminder
• Home screen and lock screen widgets
• Apple Watch app – today's name day, who celebrates next and name search
• One-tap export to your calendar, optionally repeating yearly

PRIVACY FIRST
Contacts are matched entirely on your device; only first names go to your own Apple Watch, and nothing goes anywhere else. No accounts, no ads, no analytics. The only network request is an occasional download of updated holiday data.

Based on the Czech civil calendar; the data is continuously refined.
```

**What's New (1.1):**

> New on Apple Watch: today's name day, which of your contacts celebrate next, the full-year calendar and name search. The calendar on the watch works even without your phone nearby.
