## English

**Name:** Svátek dnes

**Subtitle** (26/30): Czech name days & holidays

**Promotional text** (144/170):

> Whose name day is it today? Track Czech name days and public holidays for your contacts – all on your phone. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

**Keywords** (79/100):

```
name day,nameday,czech,calendar,svatek,jmeniny,holiday,widget,reminder,contacts
```

**Description:**

```
Whose name day is it today? Open the app – or just glance at the widget – and you know.

Svátek dnes follows the Czech civil name day calendar and matches it with your contacts, so you never forget to send your wishes. It even recognises Czech diminutives: Káťa is Kateřina, Pepa is Josef, Klárka is Klára.

WHAT IT DOES
• Today's name days and Czech public holidays on one screen
• Your contacts' name days – including nicknames and diminutives
• Manual assignment for contacts it cannot match automatically
• Full-year calendar with name search
• Public holidays and notable days, movable feasts included
• Morning notifications at your chosen time, plus an evening-before reminder
• Home screen and lock screen widgets
• One-tap export to your calendar, optionally repeating yearly

PRIVACY FIRST
Contacts are matched entirely on your device and never leave it. No accounts, no ads, no analytics. The only network request is an occasional download of updated holiday data.

Based on the Czech civil calendar; the data is continuously refined.
```
